# Store listing (draft)

## Positioning

Calm apps help you feel better. Gaman helps you get better at hard things.

Most apps in the category sell comfort: calm, gratitude, sleep. Gaman takes
the word literally. 我慢 (gaman) means bearing what is hard with patience and
dignity, which is also the Stoic idea of voluntary discomfort (Seneca's
"practise poverty", Epictetus on desire).

## Name and subtitle

- **Name**: Gaman: Stoic Endurance
- **Subtitle / short description** (≤ 30 / 80 chars):
  - "Get better at hard things"
  - "Ride out urges, choose one hard thing a day, never miss twice."

## Full description

Gaman is a daily Stoic practice of chosen discomfort.

**When an urge hits**: phone, snack, cigarette, anger, quitting. Tap
"I have an urge", name it and ride it out for ten minutes. Most urges pass.
Log whether you held, and learn your triggers.

**One trial a day**: a small hard thing chosen on purpose. Cold water at the
end of a shower, no social media, the awkward conversation. Gentle, firm and
hard levels.

**The daily loop**: a morning premeditatio (what could go wrong, and how
will you meet it?), today's trial, the one task that matters, an evening
review in the manner of Seneca.

**Never miss twice**: miss a day and it's mended in gold, kintsugi-style.
Only two misses in a row break the chain.

**Insights that measure endurance**: urges held, trials by level, your
hardest time of day.

Premium adds programs (30 days of gaman, the phone fast, Seneca's three
weeks) and endurance patterns. Monthly, yearly or lifetime.

**No AI and no ads.** No model reads your journal. Export or delete everything
from Settings.

## Claims to keep accurate

- "No AI": true while nothing calls an LLM. Revisit if one is added.
- "No ads, no third-party analytics": Crashlytics is opt-in crash reporting
  only. Keep it opt-in.
- Do **not** say "never leaves your phone": signed-in data (including
  anonymous accounts) syncs to Firestore.
- No medical claims for trials, breathing or ambient tracks. Keep the
  safety note (`trialSafetyNote`) visible wherever trials are chosen.
